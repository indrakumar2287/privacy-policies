## Introduction

IK Resume Builder respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

By using IK Resume Builder, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. User Provided Information
The app may collect information that you voluntarily provide while creating resumes, such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Education details
- Work experience
- Skills
- Project information
- Resume content

This information is used solely to generate resumes inside the app.

---

### 2. Authentication Information

If you choose to sign in using Google, the app may collect:

- Your Google account email
- Basic profile information

This is used only to identify your account and optionally store your resumes in the cloud.

Users may also choose to continue as a **Guest**, in which case no account information is collected.

---

### 3. Local Data Storage

The application stores resume data locally on your device so that you can:

- Continue editing resumes
- Save multiple resumes
- Use the app offline

This data remains on your device unless you choose to back it up using cloud features.

---

### 4. Cloud Storage (Optional)

If you sign in, resume data may be securely stored in cloud databases (such as Firebase) to allow:

- Backup of resumes
- Access across devices
- Data recovery

Cloud storage is optional and only used when the user signs in.

---

### 5. AI Text Generation

IK Resume Builder includes AI-assisted writing features that help improve resume text such as:

- Resume summaries
- Work experience descriptions
- Project descriptions

When you use these features, the text you provide may be sent to AI processing services to generate improved content.

No personal information is stored by the developer from these requests.

---

## How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used only to:

- Generate resumes
- Improve resume descriptions using AI
- Save and manage resumes
- Provide resume editing features
- Enable cloud backup (optional)

We do **not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties**.

---

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Resume information stored locally remains on your device. Cloud data is stored using secure services.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

---

## Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services including:

- Google Sign-In
- Firebase services
- AI text generation services
- Google Play services

These services may collect limited technical information as required to operate their platforms.

Please refer to their privacy policies for more details.

## Children's Privacy

IK Resume Builder is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## User Control Over Data

Users have full control over their data. You can:

- Delete resumes stored locally
- Sign out of your account
- Clear app data from device settings
